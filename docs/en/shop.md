## How to buy

1. On the Shop page, use the **categories** (All / EVM chains / TRON chain / Solana chain) or the **search box** to find a product.
2. Click a product card to see the **details**: version, size, last updated, rating, feature highlights and what the source package contains.
3. Click **“💳 Buy now”**.
4. In the popup, **choose a payment method** (shows currency + chain name).
5. The page shows the **platform payment address** (one fixed address per chain) and a QR code, and moves to the **“Waiting for payment”** screen:
   - Transfer the **amount due** exactly
   - There is a payment countdown
   - The page automatically watches for the on-chain payment, and **once confirmed the download unlocks automatically, with no manual action**
6. Once payment arrives you see **“Payment successful, source code unlocked”**; click **“⬇ Download source code”** to download it.

> 💡 The payment address is a fixed address shared across the whole site, not generated separately for each order.
> Each order's **amount due carries a unique amount tag** (for example `0.1515` is shown as `0.151537`) — that is the order's identifier.
> The system uses it to match the on-chain payment to the right order, so please **transfer the exact amount including the decimals** and do not drop the tag.

### Paid but it didn't arrive automatically?

The payment popup has a collapsible item, **“Paid but not credited automatically? Submit the transaction hash manually”**: paste the transaction hash and submit; the system verifies the payment address and amount and unlocks immediately.
